# First steps

DCS VoiceCom lets you fly with your voice. Hold a button on your HOTAS, say a command, let go. The app moves the
switch in the cockpit through DCS-BIOS.

1. Install **DCS-BIOS** (Skunkworks) in DCS.
2. Open **Settings**, click **Rebind** and press your push-to-talk button.
3. Pick your **microphone** if it is not the Windows default. Save.
4. Start DCS and jump into an aircraft. The app finds its profile by itself.
5. Hold push-to-talk, say "gear up", let go.

> **Tip:** No microphone yet? Type a phrase in the Log box and press Enter. It runs the same way.
